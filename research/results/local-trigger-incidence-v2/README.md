# Seeded outcome-unseen interface replication (v2)

Status: complete, prospectively frozen directional criterion passed.

This is the v2 replication, not the earlier v1 discovery study. It uses 20
outcome-unseen states, four interface conditions, three fixed checkpoints, and
five effective request seeds per state/condition cell: 1,200 requests total.
All 1,200 requests succeeded. All three model-level diversity/repeatability
gates passed, and every one of the 240 state/condition groups contained five
distinct semantic responses.

The registered native-schema contrast in recoverable content-only call rate was
positive at every checkpoint: Base +0.23, R2 +0.13, and R3 +0.10. The registered
directional criterion therefore passed. These are paired, finite-panel,
checkpoint-conditional interface effects. They do not establish checkpoint or
training-method superiority, improved quest performance, or generalization to
other renderers, model families, or state pools.

The routing-validity decomposition is explicitly post hoc and leaves the frozen
primary outcome unchanged. Under the same frozen schema, valid candidate counts
through either the structured or parser-recoverable text route increased from
61 to 135 (Base), 79 to 112 (R2), and 83 to 121 (R3) per 200 requests when
native tools were exposed: +37, +16.5, and +19 percentage points. The direction
was positive in all 15 checkpoint-by-sample-index slices. Of 308 recoverable
text rows, 304 were schema-valid; these are candidate actions, not evidence of
semantic appropriateness, successful execution, or state change.
